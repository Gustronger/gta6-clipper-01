A GTA 6 clip for the [gta6-clipper-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01): the Reunion moment from Trailer 2 — Lucia walks out of prison after three years, and Jason is waiting at the gate. Cut vertical (1080×1920) as a YouTube Short, with a hook caption and an end card that pitches make.fun on screen. `main.ts` copies `VIDEO.json` (the Short's address on YouTube) to `/output/VIDEO.json`, where the gate reads it.
